# UI proposal (#6)

PNG exports of the Penpot draft for issue #6 (file "New File 1" in Penpot's Drafts project, page "Screens"; it links the
published Endfield Arch library for its colours and title rule). The Endfield frames use the library's fonts: Archivo for
headings and numbers, Outfit for headlines and reading text, Space Grotesk for labels, JetBrains Mono for CVE IDs. Frame
05 uses the reader's current fonts, Newsreader and Inter. The data is the real Saturday 31 October 2026 editions,
trimmed. These are a review artifact, not assets.

- `00-tag-order.png`: the tag order for rows, at most 4 per row, most useful first, and what never shows on a row
- `01a-today-phone-panels.png`: Today on the phone, option A: one scroll with a panel per feed (lead, first section, "more in")
- `01b-today-phone-feed-tabs.png`: option B: All / Chile / Cyber / AI tabs under the date, one feed at a time; the first
  patch-now row is expanded (summary, DO NOW, Read story, open source, share, collapse)
- `01c-today-phone-top-first.png`: option C: a lead card and the top story of each feed, then headline-only lists by feed
  (Google News "Top stories" then sections)
- `01d-today-phone-panels-dark.png`: option A in a dark HUD version of Endfield
- `02a-today-desktop-panels-glance.png`: Today on desktop, option A: the Google News layout, top bar with search and
  section tabs, feed panels in a 780 px column and an "at a glance" column (patch now, your stack, blindspots, markets)
- `02b-today-desktop-three-columns.png`: option B: Chile, Cyber and AI side by side
- `03a-story-phone-quick-read.png`: a story on the phone: quick read (summary, NEXT) with the details folded
- `03b-story-phone-details-open.png`: the same with Context and Coverage & framing opened (labelled coverage bar,
  framing per group)
- `04-story-desktop.png`: a story on desktop: the quick read with Numbers open, and the thread, sources and coverage
  beside it
- `05-today-phone-top-first-light.png`: option C in today's light theme (Newsreader and Inter), to show the layout
  doesn't depend on Endfield
